How the scores get here

1 · SubmitParticipant uploads a notebook to the shared submissions repo in Submission-MilestoneN/
2 · EvaluateThe evaluator repo's GitHub Action picks up new files and scores each one against its milestone rubric, once
3 · RecordThe score is saved in results.json and never re-scored; the participant gets a comment on their commit
4 · PublishThis page and data.json are regenerated and pushed to evaluation-results/
